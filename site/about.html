About

Founder · Software Engineer

Christopher Shaw

I'm a software engineer with over 20 years of programming experience. I founded Shaw Consulting to bring practical, hands-on engineering to businesses that need software and technology they can depend on.

My approach

I grew up on a farm, where I learned persistence, resourcefulness, and the importance of solving problems instead of working around them. That mindset still guides how I work: understand the real need, make sound decisions, and build something maintainable.

My experience spans application development, system design, databases, networking, virtualization, and automation. I enjoy working across the entire stack—from APIs and data models to deployments, security, backups, and recovery.

I value straightforward solutions over unnecessary complexity. Technology should make everyday work easier, not create another problem to manage.
